TX Chat redirects Stoat to a private server:

https://txchat.duckdns.org

Stoat is downloaded and installed from the official release when it is not already on this PC. The original Stoat shortcut is left unchanged.

Stoat does not open on its own at sign-in. That start opens the public server and keeps TX Chat from switching to the private one.

To use the public server, quit TX Chat, then open Stoat from the original shortcut.
